# Data sources and trust plan

## Planned sources
- Google Places API (New): discovery enrichment, place IDs, permitted photos/attribution, and provider ratings; only via approved API and with narrow field masks.
- Official WBTC / West Bengal transport publications and licensed GTFS/open datasets: curated route import with source URL, licence, retrieval date, and verification.
- Administrator-verified local research and business submissions: application-owned market, food, and shop editorial records.
- App user contributions: reviews and corrections, visibly community-reported until moderation.

## Initial shopping discovery list
Gariahat, Hatibagan, New Market, Burrabazar, College Street, Dakshinapan, Bowbazar, Shyambazar, Esplanade/Dharmatala, and Lake Market are research candidates only. Their exact boundaries, specialties, hours, closures, transport links, and pricing require sourced verification before production claims.

## Price policy
Keep observations, not a timeless canonical price. Mark advertised, seller-reported, admin-verified, community-reported, estimated, retail, or wholesale. Compare only same product grade/unit/quantity and a freshness window documented in ranking code. No observation means no price claim.

## Licensing
Record source and licence/terms for each imported dataset and media item. Do not scrape Google Maps pages or store Google content beyond the applicable terms. Do not include unlicensed photographs.
