# Product requirements — Kolkata Explorer

## Mission
Help residents and visitors discover Kolkata attractions, independently verified local food, shopping areas and shops, transport options, and multi-stop plans. Shopping is a major product module alongside destination and food discovery.

## Users and needs
- Visitors need trustworthy place context, directions, and cost/time expectations.
- Residents need neighborhood food and market discovery, including independent businesses.
- Shoppers need product-specific market recommendations, price provenance, and clear retail/wholesale distinctions.
- Contributors can suggest corrections; administrators verify and publish factual records.

## MVP scope
1. Place search and detail vertical slice with application-curated records and optional Google Places enrichment.
2. Food/shop discovery with filters, sourced data, and app-owned reviews.
3. Shopping directory, category-to-market recommendations with explanations, comparable price observations, and up-to-three comparisons.
4. Route comparison using provider results and only verified internal transit data.
5. Cost estimates that visibly identify amount type and freshness.
6. Multi-stop itinerary persistence and admin curation workflow.

## Shopping requirements
Use expandable administrator-managed categories and subcategories. Market specialties are hypotheses until verified. Recommendations must be product-specific and explain their evidence and ranking factors. “Cheapest verified” requires recent comparable verified observations; otherwise show budget positioning or unavailable/stale status. Distinguish retail/wholesale and single/bulk quantities. Keep Google reviews separate from app reviews.

## Trust and UX
Show attribution, source, verification, freshness, and uncertainty near claims. Support denied-location fallback, loading/error/empty states, accessible controls, and English-first localization-ready copy. No important control is decorative.

## Out of scope until data exists
Live bus tracking, unsupported route modes, real-time shop stock, unverified prices/hours, and claims of market superiority without evidence.
